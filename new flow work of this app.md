CITIZEN
  → drops a pin anywhere on the map
  → fills the form (category, urgency, description, photo)
  → data saved with exact lat/lng

        ↓

ADMIN
  → sees heatmap of all citizen pins
  → notices a cluster in one street
  → draws a zone around it
  → names it (auto-suggested from address)
  → that area becomes an official zone

        ↓

URBANIST
  → only sees data INSIDE official zones
  → analyzes patterns per zone
  → plans interventions based on real citizen data
